# AnalystLab Africa — Week 2 Business Analytics Case Study

## 📊 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Programme — Week 2.

The project focuses on analyzing the Superstore Sales Dataset and developing an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.

The goal was to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can help management understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to answer the following business questions:

- What is the overall sales performance of the company?
- Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which products are the most profitable?
- What trends can be observed over time?
- What actions can management take to improve business performance?

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Regional Performance
The West region generated the highest sales at approximately **$725.46K** and the highest profit at approximately **$108.42K**.

### Category Performance
Technology was the strongest category, generating approximately **$836.15K in sales** and **$145.45K in profit**.

### Customer Segments
The Consumer segment contributed approximately **$1.16M**, representing **50.56% of total sales**.

### Furniture Profitability
Furniture generated approximately **$742.00K in sales**, but profit was only **$18.45K**, indicating an opportunity to improve margins.

### Sales Trend
Sales increased from approximately **$484.25K in 2014** to **$733.22K in 2017**, showing positive overall growth.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. Continue investing in the Technology category because of its strong sales and profitability.
2. Review pricing, discounts, and costs within the Furniture category to improve margins.
3. Develop targeted strategies to improve performance in the South region.
4. Increase engagement with Corporate and Home Office customers to diversify revenue.
5. Build on the company's positive sales trend by identifying and scaling successful strategies.
